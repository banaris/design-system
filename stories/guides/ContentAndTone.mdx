# Content and tone

The words in the catalogue's stories follow a few habits. Use them in your product so that every screen sounds like the same person.

- English, in **sentence case**: "Track a habit", not "Track A Habit".
- Short and plain. Say what happened and what to do, in the reader's terms.
- The reader is "you". Never blame them.

## Labels

A label names the thing, or the result of pressing it.

| Do                    | Don't                         |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| `Track a habit`       | `Click here to track`         |
| `Delete project`      | `Yes`                         |
| `Habit name`          | `Please enter the habit name` |
| `Email notifications` | `Notifications?`              |

- A button starts with a verb and says what it does. In a dialog that asks "Delete this project?", the confirming button says `Delete project`, not `OK`.
- A field label is a noun phrase, with no colon and no instruction. Put the instruction in a hint.
- A hint adds what the label cannot: a limit, a format, who sees it. "Up to 40 characters." "Shown to the whole team."

## Errors

An error says what is wrong and what to do about it, in that order, and is short enough to read at a glance.

| Do                                        | Don't                             |
| ----------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| `Enter a name.`                           | `Invalid input`                   |
| `Accept the terms to continue.`           | `You failed to accept the terms!` |
| `Could not load your entries. Try again.` | `Error 500`                       |

- Put the fix first when there is one: "Pick a cadence." beats "Cadence is required."
- Name the thing that failed, in the product's words, not the system's.
- No exclamation marks, no "oops", no apologising for the reader's mistake.
- Put a field's error on the field (`Field` with `error`), not in a banner above the form. Use an `Alert` when the problem is not about one field.

## Empty states

There is no empty-state component. When a `List`, a `Table` or a `Card` has nothing to show, say so in a line of `Text` where the content would be, and offer the next step.

| Do                                                 | Don't              |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------ |
| `No habits yet. Track a habit to see it here.`     | `No data`          |
| `Nothing this month. Entries you add will appear.` | _(an empty table)_ |

- Say what is missing in the product's words, then what makes something appear.
- If there is a single obvious next step, put a `Button` for it under the line.
- Do not render an empty table header with no rows; replace the whole `Table` with the message.
