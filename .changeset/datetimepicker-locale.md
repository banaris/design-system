`DateTimePicker` accepts `locale` (a BCP 47 tag for month, weekday and day names; default `"en"`, a malformed tag falls back to it), `labels` (overrides for its accessible names and visible text, with the English strings as defaults) and `firstDayOfWeek` (`0`–`6`, `0` = Sunday; by default taken from the locale). `DateTimePickerLabels` is exported.
